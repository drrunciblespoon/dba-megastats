Welcome to the DBA Megastats Project.

Here you can view stats from across all seasons of DBA to date.

Initial loading times on each page are currently quite slow, but after one search, this should improve.

You can search for any player's all time win / loss records and their pairings records on the Player and Pairings pages.

All Time DBA Records for players appear on Player Records.

You can see tables for Pokémon statistics on the Gen 9 Stats page, which can be filtered by tera and division. There are also some interesting individual Pokémon stats on the Pokémon Records page.

You can look up any Pokémon on the Look Up page, to see whether it has won titles, and its individual W/L record with each player who drafted it.

You can find a table of all our division winners and runner-ups on Hall of Fame.

Finally, all season docs are available on the Docs tab.

If you have any suggestions for improvements to this site, or requests for further statistics, please just ping or DM crème on Discord. If you've got this far, you should know where to find them!
